RAID (Redundant Array of Inexpensive Disks) is a storage technology that combines multiple physical disk drives into one or more logical units for the purposes of data redundancy, performance improvement, or both.

These notes cover the most common RAID levels, evaluating each on its capacity, reliability, and performance characteristics.
