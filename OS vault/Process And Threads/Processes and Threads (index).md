A process is one of the most fundamental concepts in a modern operating system. It represents a program in execution, serving as the unit of work. Managing processes—creating them, scheduling them on the CPU, and facilitating their communication and synchronization—is a primary responsibility of the OS kernel.

This topic explores the transition from a static program on disk to a dynamic process in memory. We will also examine threads, a more lightweight mechanism for achieving concurrency within a single process, which is critical for building responsive and efficient applications.
