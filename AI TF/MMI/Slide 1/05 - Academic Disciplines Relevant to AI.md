# Academic Disciplines Relevant to AI

Artificial Intelligence is a deeply interdisciplinary field, drawing its foundations and methodologies from several core areas.

|Discipline|Contribution to AI|
|---|---|
|**Philosophy**|Logic, methods of reasoning, the nature of the mind as a physical system, foundations of learning, language, and rationality.|
|**Mathematics**|Formal representation and proof, algorithms, computation theory (decidability and tractability), and the basis for formalizing problems.|
|**Probability / Statistics**|Modeling **uncertainty**, making optimal decisions under risk, and learning from empirical data (the foundation of modern Machine Learning).|
|**Economics**|Concepts of **utility**, decision theory, and the modeling of rational agents who seek to maximize gain.|
|**Neuroscience**|Understanding the neuron as the basic information processing unit, informing the design of neural networks and cognitive architectures.|
|**Psychology / Cognitive Science**|Research into how people behave, perceive, process cognitive information, and represent knowledge. This informs the **Thinking Humanly** approach to AI.|
|**Computer Engineering**|Providing the underlying hardware (fast computers) necessary for implementing and running AI algorithms.|
|**Control Theory**|Designing systems that maximize an objective function over time, which is critical for agents acting in continuous environments.|
|**Linguistics**|Providing principles for knowledge representation, grammar, and the structure of language, essential for Natural Language Processing (NLP).|
