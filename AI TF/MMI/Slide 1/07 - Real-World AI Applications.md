# Real-World AI Applications

AI is integrated into many facets of modern life, often in background systems that handle highly constrained, repetitive tasks.

## Everyday Systems

- **Post Office:** Automatic address recognition and sorting of mail.
    
- **Banks:** Automatic check readers, signature verification systems, and automated loan application classification.
    
- **Customer Service:** Automatic voice recognition systems (IVR).
    
- **Digital Cameras:** Automated face detection and focusing (a successful computer vision application).
    
- **Computer Games:** Intelligent characters and agents that provide realistic competition or collaboration.
    
- **The Web:** Identifying user characteristics (age, gender, location) from web surfing behavior, and automated fraud detection.
    

## AI and Language

## The Rational Agent View in Practice

Modern AI heavily relies on the **Rational Agent** view (modeling how an ideal agent _should act_). This means systems are judged by how well they perform a specific task (their utility), leading to successful applications in areas like:

- Finance (algorithmic trading, fraud detection)
    
- Medicine (diagnostic support, image analysis)
    
- Science (data mining, hypothesis generation)
