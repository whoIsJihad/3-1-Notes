# Defining Intelligence and Artificial Intelligence

## What is Intelligence?

Webster's Dictionary defines intelligence as the "capacity to learn and solve problems."

In the context of AI, it specifically refers to:

- The ability to solve **novel problems**.
    
- The ability to act **rationally**.
    
- The ability to act **like humans**.
    

## What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

**John McCarthy** (Stanford University), one of the founders of the field, defined AI as:

> "The science and engineering of making intelligent machines, especially intelligent computer programs. It is related to the similar task of using computers to understand human intelligence, but AI does not have to confine itself to methods that are biologically observable."

McCarthy also defined **Intelligence** as:

> "The computational part of the ability to achieve goals in the world."

The field of AI focuses on building and understanding intelligent **entities** or **agents**. The two main approaches are:

1. **Engineering:** Focused on building effective systems (the **rational agent** view).
    
2. **Cognitive Modeling:** Focused on understanding human intelligence and replicating it in a machine.
