# Rational Agents, Performance, and Autonomy

## Rational Agent

A **Rational Agent** selects the action that is expected to **maximize its performance measure**, given the percept sequence and its built-in knowledge.

## Performance Measure

The **Performance Measure** is the **objective criterion for success** of an agent's behavior (e.g., time to complete a course, healthy patient, minimizing cost).

## Rationality vs. Omniscience

**Rationality is distinct from omniscience** (all-knowing). No agent has infinite knowledge. Rationality means acting optimally based on the knowledge and information it _does_ have.

## Autonomy

An agent is **autonomous** if its behavior is determined primarily by its own **percepts and experience** (with the ability to learn and adapt), rather than depending solely on the designer's built-in knowledge.
